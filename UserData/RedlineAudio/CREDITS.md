# REDLINE weapon recordings

The shots use recordings by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras, and Matthew Nanney from the Free Firearm Sound Library under CC0 1.0. REDLINE cuts single takes from the lossless prepared archive and applies filtering, stereo reflections, level matching, and fades. The Flare Gun and Flak Gun use processed recordings as sound-design sources for their fictional weapons.

- [Free Firearm Sound Library](https://web.archive.org/web/20141217041948/http://freefirearmsfx.com/)
- [Prepared recordings and track metadata](https://opengameart.org/node/21826)
- [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
