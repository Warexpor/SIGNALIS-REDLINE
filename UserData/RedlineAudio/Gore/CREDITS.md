# REDLINE gore foley

REDLINE blends and trims the following CC0 recordings into two takes apiece for bullet impacts, severance, and stomps.

- [Fleshy Bone Break/Snap SFX by Zane Little Music](https://opengameart.org/content/fleshy-bone-breaksnap-sfx). Wet Break 1, 4, 7, and 10.
- [Squish Sounds Effects by EZduzziteh](https://opengameart.org/content/squish-sounds-effects). squish_02, squish_04, squish_06, and squishsplat_impact.
